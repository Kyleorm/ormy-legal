4. What Ormy is

Ormy is a proactive reminder assistant. You tell it about things you need to remember — appointments, birthdays, medication, bin day, deadlines — and it stores them and sends you notifications, nudges and check-ins so they are less likely to be forgotten.

Ormy uses artificial intelligence to hold conversations with you, to interpret what you tell it, and to create and manage reminders on your behalf.

5. What Ormy is not — please read this

Ormy is a helpful assistant. It is not a safety-critical system, and you must not treat it as one.

Do not rely on Ormy as your only safeguard for anything affecting your health, safety, money, legal position or livelihood. That includes — but is not limited to — medication doses, hospital and clinic appointments, court and tribunal dates, visa and immigration deadlines, insurance renewals, tax filings, exam dates, childcare and school pick-ups, and travel departures.

Anything in that category should always have a second, independent safeguard — a calendar alarm, a paper note, a person, a pill organiser. Ormy is designed to be the reminder that reaches you, not the only one that exists.

6. Artificial intelligence and its limits

Ormy is powered by large language models supplied by Anthropic. You should understand the following before relying on anything it produces:

  • AI output can be wrong. It can misunderstand you, invent details that were never said, and state incorrect things with complete confidence.
  • AI can get dates and times wrong. When Ormy creates a reminder from something you typed or said, it is interpreting your words. It may pick the wrong day, the wrong time, the wrong repeat pattern or the wrong time zone.
  • You are responsible for checking. Reminders Ormy has created for you are visible in the app, and where the app has flagged one as needing review or as incomplete, that flag means what it says. Please check anything that matters.
  • AI output is not deterministic. The same question can produce different answers on different days.

Content you send to Ormy is transmitted to Anthropic for the sole purpose of generating a response. Under our agreement with Anthropic, your content is not used to train their models. See the Privacy Policy and the sub-processor list for the detail.
