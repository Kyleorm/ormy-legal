3. What we do not use

Named explicitly, because their absence is the point:

  • No advertising or attribution networks — no Meta SDK, no Google Ads, no AppsFlyer, no Adjust.
  • No behavioural analytics platform collecting your in-app activity.
  • No data brokers or enrichment services.
  • No AI training on your data, by us or by any provider above.

4. How we choose and check providers

Before a provider gets access to personal data we confirm that it:

  • will sign a data processing agreement meeting Article 28 of the UK and EU GDPR;
  • offers a lawful transfer mechanism for data leaving the UK and EEA;
  • encrypts data in transit and at rest;
  • publishes its own security posture and breach notification commitments;
  • is contractually barred from using our users' data for its own purposes.

We review this list at least annually, and whenever we change any part of the stack.

5. Changes to this list

Adding or replacing a sub-processor is a change to how your data is handled, so we treat it as one.

  • This page is updated before a new provider begins processing.
  • Business customers with a signed Data Processing Agreement receive at least 30 days' written notice and may object on reasonable data protection grounds.
  • Consumers who object to a new provider can delete their account at any time from Settings.
