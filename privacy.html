Privacy Policy

Ormy — last updated 10 July 2026

Ormy is a proactive AI reminder assistant operated by Turbolt ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data we collect, why, and what we do with it. The short version: we collect only what the app needs to work, we never sell your data, and you can delete everything at any time.

What we collect

  • Account details — your name and email address, used to sign you in. Authentication is handled by Clerk.
  • Your reminders and events — the appointments, birthdays, tasks and dates you ask Ormy to remember, stored securely in our database (Convex) so they sync and so notifications can be sent.
  • Chat messages — what you say to Ormy, stored so it can remember context across conversations. Messages are processed by Anthropic's Claude AI to generate responses. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models.
  • Push notification token — a device identifier needed to deliver reminders to your phone.
  • Subscription status — handled by Apple and RevenueCat. We never see your payment details.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • We don't show ads or share data with advertisers.
  • We don't read your device calendar, contacts, or location.
  • We don't use your conversations to train AI models.

Who your data is shared with

Only the service providers that make the app work: Clerk (sign-in), Convex (database), Anthropic (AI responses), Expo (push notification delivery), and RevenueCat/Apple (subscriptions). Each receives only what it needs for its job.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept while your account is active. Delete your account and your reminders, chat history and profile are permanently removed from our systems.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you at any time. UK/EU users have these rights under GDPR. Just email us.

Children

Ormy is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll let you know in the app.
